Emergency Water Heater Leak Cleanup in South Windsor, CT

A failed water heater tank can release dozens of gallons onto a basement or utility room floor. Shut off the power or gas and the cold water supply, then call the 24/7 line.

(844) 950-6900

A leaking water heater is a source-specific emergency. A tank holds 40 to 80 gallons in most homes, and a rusted-through tank can empty onto the floor in minutes. A slow leak from a fitting is quieter but can soak a closet or basement wall for days.

Right now, turn off the power to an electric heater at the breaker or set a gas heater to pilot or off. Close the cold water supply valve on top of the tank. Then call the 24/7 line. Same-day response is available when crews are open.

Water heater flood: steps in order

  1. Turn off power at the breaker for an electric unit. For gas, turn the control to off and leave the room if you smell gas.
  2. Close the cold water inlet valve on the pipe above the tank.
  3. Do not step in water near the heater if the floor is wet and power is on. Call the electric utility or 911 if you are unsure.
  4. Open a hot faucet upstairs to relieve pressure and let the tank drain.
  5. Move stored boxes and electronics off the floor.
  6. Call (844) 950-6900.

Why tanks fail

Tank water heaters corrode from the inside. Sediment builds on the bottom, the anode rod wears out and the steel fails at a seam or fitting. Many tanks are replaced after about 10 years, and older units leak more often.

Other leaks come from the pressure relief valve, the drain valve, the supply connectors or a failed expansion tank. A relief valve that discharges is working as designed, so a repeated discharge points to pressure or temperature problems rather than a failed tank.

The leak source decides the repair. Water in the room always needs extraction and drying.

Cleanup after the tank leak

The crew extracts standing water and checks the floor, the wall bases and anything stored nearby. In finished basements, carpet and pad are the biggest losses. Cabinets, drywall and framing near the heater need moisture readings.

Water that came from a clean tank is generally Category 1 at the start, so quick action often saves drywall and trim. If the water sat for more than a day or two, it can move to Category 2, and more removal is needed. See structural drying for how the dry-down works.

Placement matters in South Windsor homes

A water heater in a basement can leak unseen behind a stack of boxes. A heater in a finished basement closet is more likely to wet carpet and drywall. A heater on a raised pad with a drain pan limits the spread.

If the leak reached the floor of a finished basement, read basement water damage cleanup. For wet drywall and baseboards, see wall and drywall water damage repair. The same steps apply in nearby East Windsor.

What to do first when a water heater leaks

  1. Turn off the power or gas to the heater at its switch or valve.
  2. Close the cold water supply valve above the tank.
  3. Drain only if you can do it safely and the water is not scalding.
  4. Do not touch wiring while standing in water.
  5. Call a plumber for the repair and a crew for the drying.

Tank water can be hot and may carry sediment. Crews classify water and plan drying under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration.

Water heater leak questions

Do you handle water heater leak emergencies at night or on weekends in South Windsor?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7. Tanks often fail overnight. Call after you shut off power or gas and the cold water supply. Arrival times are not guaranteed.

Should I shut off the gas or electric first?

Shut off whichever fuel the unit uses so no burner or element runs without water. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 and the gas utility. Do not step in water if the power is on and you cannot confirm it is off.

Is a leaking water heater covered by insurance?

The water damage from a sudden failure is often covered, while the heater itself and slow leaks may not be. Check your policy or ask your insurer.

Can I dry a small leak myself?

A few cups on a concrete floor can be wiped up and fanned dry. If water reached carpet, drywall or cabinets, moisture can stay behind. A moisture meter shows whether it is really dry.
