Emergency Storm Water Damage Cleanup in South Windsor, CT

Storm water damage is rain and wind driving water through the roof, windows, siding and doors. Tarp what you can, stay away from downed lines, and call the 24/7 line for drying and cleanup.

(844) 950-6900

Storm damage lets water in through the building envelope. Wind lifts shingles, a limb punches a hole in a roof, rain drives sideways through a failed window seal and gutters overflow behind siding. This page covers that kind of damage. Rising water from rivers and groundwater is a flood, and it has its own page on flood damage cleanup.

If you can do it safely, tarp the opening and move belongings away from wet spots. Stay away from downed power lines and call the utility. The 24/7 line is open, and same-day response is available when crews can travel safely.

Storm emergency: what to do while it is still raining

  1. Stay out of rooms with sagging ceilings or active drips near light fixtures.
  2. Put buckets under drips and move electronics and papers.
  3. Shut off power to a wet room only if the breaker is dry and reachable.
  4. Photograph the opening, the water and the damage before you cover anything.
  5. Call (844) 950-6900, and call your insurer to open a claim.

Dry the structure before you rebuild

The crew checks every room that touched the wet area with a moisture meter and thermal camera. Wet insulation and soft drywall come out. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry the framing and the building envelope.

A roofer or contractor makes permanent exterior repairs, while restoration removes water and dries the interior. Both trades usually work on the same storm loss. See structural drying for how dry targets are set.

Insurance after a storm

Wind and rain damage that comes through a storm opening is often covered under homeowners insurance, while flood is generally excluded. The Connecticut Insurance Department advises photographing and videoing damage before cleanup, keeping receipts for temporary repairs and reporting claims promptly. It also says policyholders should prevent further damage, such as covering a damaged roof, and should not feel pushed to accept a settlement.

Keep wet materials until the adjuster has seen them when you can. Ask for scope and price in writing before work starts.

Storm water damage questions

Do you handle storm water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in South Windsor?

Yes. The line is open 24/7. After a widespread storm, call volume and road conditions affect timing, so arrival times are not guaranteed. Call early and say whether power is out.

Should I tarp the roof myself?

Only if you can do it safely from the ground or a stable position. Never climb a wet or icy roof. A roofer can tarp the damage, and a temporary cover helps your claim by limiting further damage.

Is storm damage the same as flood damage?

No. Storm damage is water entering through the building from wind and rain. Flood is water rising from outside, and insurers usually treat it differently.

Will insurance cover storm water damage?

Often yes when water enters through a storm-created opening, though policies differ. Ask your insurer, document everything and keep receipts.
