What Homeowners Can Safely Dry Themselves and What Needs a Pro: South Windsor, CT

You can handle a small spill of clean water on a hard floor with towels and fans. Anything involving sewage, flood water, wet drywall, carpet pad, ceilings or more than a day of water needs a meter and equipment. When in doubt, check with a moisture meter.

A simple decision test

When in doubt, check with a moisture meter. Surface dryness does not prove the materials behind it are dry.

Carpet and pad soakedNoPad holds water, subfloor stays wet
Wet drywall or baseboardsNoWater is inside the wall

What you can do safely right now

  1. Stop the source and shut off the main valve if a pipe is leaking.
  2. Turn off power to a wet room only if the panel is dry and reachable.
  3. Soak up standing clean water with towels and a wet vacuum plugged into a dry outlet.
  4. Lift small items and furniture legs off wet carpet.
  5. Open windows and run fans if the weather allows.
  6. Photograph the damage.

Where DIY drying fails

Water moves into places a fan cannot reach. It runs down wall cavities, under baseboards and below flooring. A fan can dry the surface and leave the framing wet. Moisture left inside a wall can feed mold.

A dehumidifier from a hardware store helps, but a household unit is a small fraction of the capacity professional low-grain units provide. Without a meter, you cannot know when to stop.

Renting or buying a meter

A basic moisture meter costs little and tells you whether a wall or floor reads wet. Take readings in a dry room first so you know what normal looks like for your house. Then check the wet area and its edges.

If readings stay high after two or three days of fans and a dehumidifier, call for help. That is a sign water is trapped.

South Windsor note: when a permit may be involved

Drying alone does not need a permit. Rebuilding walls, replacing electrical work or moving plumbing may. The South Windsor Building Department can tell you what applies. Its phone number is (860) 644-2511, extension 2330, and it says to call to check whether a project needs a permit or inspections.

If your loss followed a storm, the Capitol Region hazard mitigation plan records power outages of four to five days after the August 2020 storm. Never use a generator indoors, and do not run electrical equipment in wet rooms.

Safety points that apply to everyone

  • Never touch electrical equipment while standing in water.
  • Do not use a household vacuum on wet floors unless it is rated for wet pickup.
  • Do not use a generator indoors or in an attached garage.
  • Wear boots and gloves if water is not clearly clean.
  • Keep children and pets out of wet areas.

After a flood or sewage backup, do not dry it yourself. See flood damage cleanup and sewage backup cleanup. For wet carpet, see carpet water damage restoration. The same guidance applies in Windsor Locks and Broad Brook.

A weekend example: a small dishwasher leak

A dishwasher leaks a cup of clean water onto a tile kitchen floor. You catch it within minutes. Towels pick it up, you wipe the area dry and run a fan for an hour. A meter reading at the toe-kick shows normal. That is a reasonable DIY job.

Now change one fact. The leak ran overnight and the cabinet base is swollen. The floor looks dry on top, but the cabinet and subfloor are wet. That needs a meter, drying equipment and probably removal of the toe-kick.

How long does DIY drying take

There is no single answer. Thin materials like carpet dry faster than thick ones like hardwood, concrete or framing. Weather and room size matter too. Instead of counting days, use readings. When a wet area reads the same as a dry reference area, drying is done.

If readings are still high after a few days of steady fans and a dehumidifier, there is moisture trapped somewhere. That is the time to call.

Any of these after a few days means DIY drying is not finishing the job.
SignWhat it may mean
Musty smell after dryingMoisture is still present in a hidden spot

Equipment homeowners ask about

Box fans move air, which helps surface drying but does little for wet framing. A wet vacuum pulls up standing water, but only a small amount at a time. A household dehumidifier lowers room humidity, though it cannot pull water out of thick materials quickly.

Professional equipment is sized to the room and the material. Air movers push air along surfaces and into cavities. Low-grain dehumidifiers dry the air deeply. A thermal camera shows where to look, and a meter confirms the numbers.

Keep a log while you dry

Write down the date, time and a reading from the meter for each wet spot. Note which fans are running and what the room humidity is. A simple log shows whether drying is working or stalled.

If your insurer asks what you did, the log answers. It also helps a professional pick up where you left off, without repeating work.

Photograph the area each day from the same angle. Comparing pictures from day one and day three makes progress easy to see.

DIY drying questions

How long can I wait before calling a pro?

Water starts to soak into drywall, wood and pad within hours. Many standards treat a day or two as the window where drying is far easier, so call sooner.

Will a household dehumidifier dry a flooded basement?

It helps with humidity, but it is not enough alone for wet walls, floors or carpet pad.

Is a wet vacuum enough for standing water?

For a small clean spill, yes. For deeper water, contaminated water or water near electrical equipment, call a crew.

Does South Windsor require a permit to dry a house?

Drying does not. Rebuilding may. Call the Building Department at (860) 644-2511, extension 2330.
