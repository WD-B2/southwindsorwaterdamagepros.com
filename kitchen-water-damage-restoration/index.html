Kitchen Water Damage Restoration in South Windsor, CT

Kitchens hold sinks, dishwashers, fridges and a lot of cabinetry. Water under the sink or behind a unit swells wood and loosens floors. Call the 24/7 line to dry the kitchen before cabinets are lost.

(844) 950-6900

Kitchen water damage usually starts small. A drip from a sink trap, a loose supply line or a dishwasher gasket leaks into a cabinet base and swells particleboard. By the time the door will not close or the floor feels spongy, water has been there a while.

Shut off the supply valve under the sink or at the main, empty the cabinet and photograph the damage. Then call the 24/7 line. This page is about cabinets, counters and the kitchen floor. The leaking appliance itself is covered under appliance leak water damage.

Signs the damage is bigger than the leak

  • Cabinet bases swelling, peeling or crumbling at the bottom edge.
  • Toe kicks that look warped or stained.
  • Floor planks lifting near the sink or dishwasher.
  • Musty smell when you open a cabinet.
  • Dark stains on the back of the sink base.
  • Countertop seams lifting.

What can be saved and what cannot

Solid wood cabinets and plywood boxes often dry out if the water was clean and the exposure was short. Particleboard and medium-density fiberboard swell and crumble when wet, and they usually need replacing. Laminate countertops delaminate at wet edges, and stone and solid surface usually survive.

Cabinet face frames and doors can be saved even when the boxes are replaced. A moisture meter decides, since surface dryness does not prove the core is dry.

How a kitchen restoration is done

The crew removes the toe kick and the base of affected cabinets so air can reach the wall and floor. Moisture mapping shows how far water traveled under the cabinets. Air movers and a dehumidifier run, and drying equipment can be set to pull air through the cabinet cavity.

If the subfloor is wet, it is dried before new flooring goes down. If water reached the room below, check ceiling water damage repair. Wet drywall behind the kitchen is covered under wall and drywall water damage repair.

Cost factors

  • Number of cabinet boxes wet and what they are made of.
  • Countertop and backsplash removal if access is needed.
  • Flooring type and subfloor condition.
  • Drying days.
  • Rebuild scope, such as cabinets, counters and paint.

Sudden leaks are often covered under homeowners insurance, while slow leaks under a sink are often excluded. Ask your insurer and keep the failed part. Scope and price are explained before work starts. Call (844) 950-6900 for your own number.

South Windsor kitchens and the shutoff you need

Know where the main water valve is, since a kitchen leak at night can run for hours. Check the supply lines under the sink and dishwasher twice a year for corrosion. Replace rubber hoses and compression fittings that look crusty.

If the leak sat for weeks, mold may be growing on the back of the cabinet. Mold testing and removal follow the S520 standard under mold remediation. Nearby East Windsor homes follow the same checks.

What to check after a kitchen leak is stopped

Open the cabinet doors and look at the back wall and the floor of each unit. Check under the dishwasher and refrigerator for a damp subfloor. Pull the toe-kick on a wet cabinet run, because water travels along it.

A moisture meter helps you decide what is truly dry. If readings stay high near the sink wall after a day of airflow, call for help before the cabinets are refinished or the floor is sealed.

What to do first after a kitchen leak

  1. Close the shutoff valve under the sink or behind the appliance.
  2. Unplug small appliances only if your hands and floor are dry.
  3. Empty the base cabinet and open the doors for airflow.
  4. Mop clean water and lift mats.
  5. Photograph the cabinet, the floor and the source.

Kitchen water damage questions

Can wet kitchen cabinets be saved?

Sometimes. Solid wood and plywood often dry if the exposure was short and the water was clean. Particleboard usually does not survive. A moisture meter decides.

How long does it take to dry a kitchen?

Often three to five days for a localized leak, longer if the subfloor or the room below is wet. Daily readings show progress.

Do I need to remove the countertop?

Not always. Many jobs dry cabinets from the toe kick and the front. If the countertop is bonded to a wet base, it may need to be lifted.

Is the 24/7 line open for kitchen leaks?
